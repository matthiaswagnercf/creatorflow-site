Services

Four kinds of systems. On the call I tell you which one fits.

CRM & sales automation

Every lead gets a reply and a follow-up, and shows up in your CRM.

  • First reply to inbound leads within a minute, in your wording
  • Routing, deduplication and enrichment when a lead arrives
  • Follow-ups that stop as soon as someone replies
  • A weekly pipeline sheet

AI lead generation

Cold outbound to accounts that look like your best customers.

  • Lists built from signals, like job posts or Google Maps
  • The decision-maker's email, found and verified where it exists
  • Sending domains and warmup kept apart from your main domain
  • Replies handed to your CRM or your calendar

AI automation

The repetitive work between your tools, done in the background.

  • A map of the workflow before anything is built
  • Your forms, inbox, sheets, CRM and calendar talking to each other
  • Tested and documented, and your team owns it

Custom AI agents

One agent, one job. A person on your team approves what it sends.

  • Support and qualification replies, drafted or sent
  • Answers from your own documents and past tickets
  • Every action is logged, and you approve the risky ones
