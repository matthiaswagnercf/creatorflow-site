What happens to a lead at 9pm

Someone fills your form, clicks your ad, or replies to your campaign. The one person who answers is on another call, or it's Saturday. The lead waits four hours, sometimes until Monday. By then they've filled two other forms and the intent that made them write is gone.

You already paid for that lead once in ad spend. You pay for it a second time in the wait. The person you're hiring will fix this nine to five, once they're ramped. I cover the rest of the clock, and the weeks before they start.
